# AENV IPTV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

AENV IPTV is a media player. It is designed to work without collecting any
personal data.

WHAT THE APP STORES
- Playlists you add (names, URLs and, for Xtream Codes accounts, server,
  username and password), your favorites, recently watched channels and your
  language setting.
- This data is stored only on your device, in %AppData%\AenvIPTV. If Microsoft
  OneDrive is set up on your PC, the library file is stored in your own
  OneDrive folder (OneDrive\AenvIPTV) so it syncs between your computers.
- Error logs (crash.log) are written locally and never sent anywhere.

WHAT THE APP SENDS
- The app connects only to the playlist, EPG and streaming servers that YOU
  enter. These servers receive the requests needed to load and play content
  (for example your IP address and, for Xtream Codes, your login).
  Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or telemetry, and
  sends no data to the developer.

YOUR CONTROL
- Remove a playlist in the app to delete its data, or delete the AenvIPTV
  folders listed above. Uninstalling the app removes the program files.

CHILDREN
- The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

CHANGES AND CONTACT
- Changes to this policy will be published with new versions of the app.
- Questions: use the support contact shown on the app's Microsoft Store page
  or open an issue in the project's repository.
